About CodeWithMe

CodeWithMe is a coding and collaborative learning platform designed to help students and developers learn, practice, and work together.

What is CodeWithMe?

CodeWithMe provides tools for coding practice, programming discussions, collaborative study rooms, and AI-powered programming assistance.

Our Features

  • AI-powered coding assistance.
  • Collaborative coding and study rooms.
  • Real-time room chat.
  • Programming discussions and problem solving.
  • Learning resources for students and developers.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make programming practice and collaborative learning easier and more accessible for students and developers.
